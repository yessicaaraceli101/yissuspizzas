Overview

TableTime is a prototype that simulates a restaurant discovery, booking, and delivery flow. It is not connected to a backend service. Most behavior is mock logic running in your browser.

Data We Collect

We do not intentionally collect or process real personal data. For demonstration, the following values may be stored in your browser’s localStorage:

  • Basic checkout form inputs (e.g., name/email you typed for the demo)
  • Cart contents, mock “orders,” and booking details
  • UI settings (e.g., dark/light theme, filters)

These values remain on your device and are not sent to a server by this demo.

How We Use Data

Any locally stored values are used only to render the demo experience (e.g., showing a mock order confirmation). They have no real-world effect and are not used for analytics, advertising, or profiling.

Storage & Retention

All demo data is stored client-side in your browser and persists until you clear it. You can remove demo data anytime by clearing your browser’s site data or storage.

Cookies & Local Storage

This demo may use localStorage (and possibly cookies) to remember simple preferences (e.g., theme) and mock order state. No sensitive data should be entered here.

Third-Party Services

The demo UI may load third-party libraries via CDNs and placeholder image services. Those providers have their own policies; review them if needed.

Security

This is a demo environment and not hardened for production. Do not enter real personal, financial, or sensitive information.

Children’s Privacy

This demo is not intended for children. Do not submit any information about minors.

Changes to This Policy

We may update or remove this demo at any time without notice. Updates to this policy will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above.
